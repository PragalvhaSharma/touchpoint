The booth that remembers you.

Touchpoint is an AI booth recruiter. It knows you when you walk up, talks about what you've actually built, points you to the people in the room worth meeting, and follows up after you leave with an email that is about you.

"Hi, what's your name, what are you studying?" Every booth, every student, forty times a day. By Monday nobody remembers who said what.

Sponsors come to hackathons to meet builders, then spend the weekend asking the same three questions and leave with a stack of forgotten names. Touchpoint does the homework before you reach the table, so the first minute is about your work and the conversation keeps going after you walk away.

How it works

Four moments, one conversation.

From walking up to the booth to a follow-up in your inbox, and nothing is sent until you confirm it's really you.

01

Recognise

A booth camera spots you as you walk up and matches you against a researched profile of the hackers in the room: your projects, your stack, what you're looking for.

OpenCV · Python camera service
02

Talk

A real-time voice agent greets you by what you've built, keeps the conversation going, and shows who else at the event you should meet next.

OpenAI Realtime · Flue agents
03

Follow up

When you leave, a TinyFish browser agent reads the sponsor's live careers site and drafts a follow-up with the roles that fit what you talked about.

TinyFish web agent
04

Confirm

Before anything reaches you, you confirm it's you with a passkey: one Face ID tap, no password, no form.

Auth0 passkeys

After the booth

A follow-up that knows what you talked about.

No "Thanks for stopping by!" blast. Touchpoint remembers the conversation and checks what the company is hiring for today.

  • Live roles. Pulled from the sponsor's careers site when the email is written, not from last month's PDF.
  • Your words. It refers back to the project and interests you mentioned at the booth.
  • Your call. A Face ID passkey confirms it's you before anything is sent.
