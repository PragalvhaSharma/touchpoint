The plan: one project, many tracks

OptionProjectTracks it hits
A · pick thisClinic front-desk agent. Staff type a patient's plan, and the agent logs into insurer portals, checks physio or dental coverage, and fills in the claim.TinyFish, Sun Life, Gemini, Auth0, Vultr, GoDaddy, Entire (+ Tiger Data if we log claim history)
B · backup"Future Me" money agent. It compares TFSA, FHSA and savings rates across real bank sites, then shows a Gen Z user their projected balance at 30.Canada Life, TinyFish, Gemini, Auth0, Vultr, GoDaddy, Tiger Data (rate history)

  • Why A: the browser agent is the core of the TinyFish track, and you've built agents before (GrokHunt, Paintbot). Insurer portal work is a real business job, which TinyFish asks for, and a health problem, which Sun Life asks for.
  • A or B: they're about equally strong. Pick A for the health angle or B for the finance angle. Each one fits one sponsor's prompt exactly.

Fine print that affects us

  • Stacking is allowed. The rules say to "select the sponsor prize tracks you want to enter" on both the live site and Devpost, and the tracks and team members must match on both. I found no cap in the rules or Slack. Confirm in #0-ask-organizers if it matters.
  • Team: up to 4 people, and all must be accepted Hack Western 13 participants who are present on site. One project per team.
  • The TinyFish track needs a public post. You'd post the demo on X or LinkedIn yourself. I won't post for you. One hacker in #spon-tinyfish reports the student credits sign-up isn't working, so claim credits early.
  • Sponsors without a posted track: Scotiabank (title sponsor), Manulife, Accenture, Autodesk, Commure, CSE, Shopify, StarTech and UBIT have Slack channels with no challenge posted yet. Watch them, since a late track could add one more stack.
