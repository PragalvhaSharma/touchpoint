Touchpoint

Turn someone’s background into a conversation worth continuing.

A booth connection assistant that brings relevant attendee context into the introduction and carries the conversation into a useful, personalized follow-up.

One product story: context before, continuity after

Who we serve

Sponsor representatives at busy booths

The problem: representatives start conversations with little context, then lose the details needed to follow through. Attendees repeat introductions and receive generic messages afterward.

The promise: arrive informed, find a relevant reason to connect, and leave with a next step worth following up on.

What Touchpoint does

Connect the person to the conversation

Touchpoint assembles relevant, attendee-approved background from Slack and project links. The attendee confirms their profile; the sponsor sees useful context, adds a brief conversation note, and receives a resource-backed follow-up draft.

Positioning: a sponsor’s connection and follow-through assistant. Collected background is the input; a useful ongoing relationship is the outcome.

The product loop: confirmed background → relevant introduction → captured conversation → researched resource → reviewed follow-up. Each step should support the same visitor interaction.

The pitch we tell every judge

Touchpoint turns someone’s background into a conversation worth continuing. We bring attendee-approved Slack and project context into sponsor booth conversations, then help sponsors follow through with relevant resources and personalized messages.

Build status: this page records target tracks and proposed features, not proof that integrations are complete. Identify the attendee through a participant-controlled badge, QR code, or account check-in, and let them confirm shared information. Profile context does not replace a note about what was actually discussed. No attendee data is included here.

Primary requirements and the Canada Life alternative

01 · Technology track

Gemini API

Strong fit · Both directions

What the challenge asks

Build an AI-powered application using the Gemini API. The supplied description does not prescribe a specific problem area.

Our use

Send approved Slack excerpts, project information, sponsor capabilities, and conversation notes to Gemini. Receive a structured brief, an explained match, or a follow-up draft.

Concrete example

A student explicitly asks for embedded-systems help. Gemini identifies the question and explains why a particular sponsor resource is relevant, citing the supplied evidence.

Show the judges

  • Two different attendee requests produce meaningfully different outputs.
  • Every profile claim can be traced to a source.
  • The attendee can correct the brief; missing facts remain unknown.

Required distinction

Integrate the API into the product. Using Gemini only for brainstorming or coding is not the proposed product integration.

Listed prize: MLH swag kits.

02 · Browser automation track

TinyFish

Strong fit · Complete a real job

What the challenge asks

An “AI employee” that completes a browser-based job end to end on live websites. Judges emphasize actual completion, robustness, and smart use of TinyFish.

Our use

A sponsor follow-up assistant researches the requested resource on the sponsor’s live website and prepares the completed follow-up record in a designated browser workspace.

Concrete example

“Find the official beginner resource relevant to this visitor’s question, capture its link and details, and prepare a follow-up draft in our team workspace.”

Show the judges

  • The assignment, live navigation, and completed record.
  • Visible progress and an honest blocked/failed state.
  • Sources and a message review step before sending.

Qualification detail in the brief

Post the demo on X or LinkedIn and tag TinyFish. Complete that separately; creating a demo does not publish it.

Listed prize: $75 gift card + $100 TinyFish credits per team member, plus a team 1:1 with an engineering lead. Student credits: signup; documentation.

Alternative · Outside the current core scope

Canada Life Future Me

Strong only with a focused adaptation

What the challenge asks

Help Gen Z take meaningful action toward their financial future through motivation, visualization, education, behavioral nudges, or innovation.

Our use

A drop-in planning concierge asks for a goal, timeframe, obstacle, and any circumstances the visitor chooses to provide. It creates an educational takeaway and helps the visitor choose one action.

Concrete example

A student wants to prepare for life after graduation. They receive a relevant planning explanation, a researched educational resource, and an action they explicitly select.

Show the judges

  • Two user-chosen goals lead to different plans.
  • The visitor leaves with a concrete next step.
  • The explanation makes the future feel actionable.

Scope boundary

Do not infer income, debt, risk tolerance, or financial needs from faces or Slack posts. Use direct user input. Keep the prototype about education and planning support.

Listed prize: $100 Visa prepaid gift card per winning team member.

Vultr hosts your application and job worker. Your service stores the interaction, calls Gemini and TinyFish, and tracks queued, running, draft-ready, approved, sent, or failed work. Gemini and TinyFish remain separate services. A cloud GPU is not needed merely to call their APIs.

Sponsor perspective

Assignment: “Prepare useful follow-ups for the visitors who requested resources.”

Use the confirmed profile and booth note, find appropriate official resources, draft the message, and record the result. This is the clearest repetitive employee task for the TinyFish demonstration.

Student perspective

Assignment: “Help me follow through after meeting this sponsor.”

Find the program or resource mentioned, connect it to the student’s stated goal, and prepare a thank-you message with a specific next step. Choose one perspective for the weekend MVP.

Gemini drafting a message and an email API sending it would show automation, but would give TinyFish little meaningful browser work. Validate the chosen websites and destination workspace early. External messages require review or an explicitly authorized sending workflow.

The Touchpoint demo

Sponsor connections is our selected direction. The financial-planning view is retained only as an alternative scope, not an additional feature commitment. These are planning views, not live product demos.

Recommended bundle

Gemini + TinyFish, supported by Auth0 and Vultr

  1. Use two volunteer-approved attendee profiles with source-linked Slack excerpts.
  2. Check in an attendee and confirm their brief.
  3. Capture one concrete request or sponsor conversation note.
  4. Have TinyFish find the matching resource on a live sponsor website.
  5. Generate a grounded draft and save the completed follow-up record.
  6. Show approval and status. Label any simulated delivery clearly.

Success measures: supported profile facts, relevant resources, complete job outcomes, actual processing time, and whether the follow-up delivers the requested next step. Measure results rather than claiming an untested improvement.

Alternative bundle

Canada Life + Gemini + TinyFish, supported by Auth0 and Vultr

  1. Ask the visitor to choose a future goal and provide relevant circumstances directly.
  2. Confirm any optional background context they want to reuse.
  3. Generate an educational explanation and possible next actions.
  4. Use TinyFish to find an appropriate live educational resource and prepare the takeaway record.
  5. Have the visitor select and confirm one next step.
  6. Show the plan and optional reminder choice. Keep the demo educational.

Success measures: the visitor can explain their next action, the resource supports their stated goal, and the plan uses confirmed inputs. Slack collection is supporting context in this version.
